# Client assets and editorial update

## Portraits
Ten supplied portraits were standardized to WebP. Unzip `earndeck-assets-ready.zip` into the repository root and commit the resulting `public/team/*.webp` files. The TeamGrid reads these images by slug and will fall back to initials when an asset is missing.

## Licence display
The supplied artwork is available as `public/brand/license.webp` in the same asset package. It appears below the footer brand and on the Certificates page when copied in.

## Editorial cautions
- Team titles and qualifications follow user-supplied text; review credentials and spelling before launch.
- Old /marine-careers and /marine-certificates URLs were inspected but returned a JavaScript shell, so exact original copy and documentary details could not be recovered.
- Careers page does not claim any live vacancy; certificates page does not claim current accreditation or validity without documents.
- Verify licence status, permission to display institutional logos, and original certifying authorities before launch.
